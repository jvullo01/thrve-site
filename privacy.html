Privacy Policy

THRVE · Your workouts stay on your iPhone

The short version

Data Not Collected

THRVE doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. There are no third-party tools or SDKs in the app, and there are no servers for it to talk to. Everything you record or import stays on your iPhone.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you allow it, THRVE reads your workouts (with their routes, heart rate, distance and energy) and your steps so they show up in your history. If you turn on Save to Apple Health in Settings (off by default), THRVE adds the workouts you record in THRVE to Apple Health.

Health data stays on your iPhone. It is never sent to us or anyone else, never stored in iCloud by THRVE, and never used for advertising, marketing or data mining. You can change or remove Health access any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Location: used while the Record screen is open, to show where you are and when GPS is ready (this live view is not saved), and while you record a workout you started, to draw your route and measure distance and pace. THRVE asks only for "While Using the App" access. Routes are saved on your iPhone.
  • Motion & Fitness: used to show today's steps from your iPhone's motion sensors.
  • Bluetooth: used only to connect heart rate straps and speed or cadence sensors you choose to pair.
  • Imports: Strava archives and GPX, TCX and FIT files you pick are read on your iPhone.
  • Settings and goals: units, goals, spoken splits and similar preferences.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Every permission is optional; if you say no, the rest of the app keeps working.

On-device intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on, the Coach summary and plain-English history search run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device model. They never use a server, including Apple's Private Cloud Compute, and nothing you ask is sent anywhere. Siri and Shortcuts actions also run on your iPhone.

Trail map

THRVE's Pennsylvania trail lines, including named hiking trails, cycleways and rail trails from OpenStreetMap, are built into the app and read on your iPhone. The app makes no network calls to show them, downloads nothing, and never sends your location anywhere. Trail data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL (openstreetmap.org/copyright).

Exporting and deleting

You can export any activity as GPX, or everything as a .zip (History → Export all), through the share sheet. Where an export goes is up to you.

Deleting THRVE removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Workouts you chose to save to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

Purchases

If THRVE offers an optional in-app purchase, Apple handles the payment. We never see your name, email, card or Apple Account details.

Children

THRVE is made for a general audience. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it doesn't collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If THRVE ever starts collecting anything, this policy and the App Store privacy label will say so first.
