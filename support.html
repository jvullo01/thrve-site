Support

THRVE · Every Move Counts.

Contact

Email jvappsupport+thrve@proton.me. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

My route stops when the screen locks

In iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → THRVE, choose While Using the App and turn on Precise Location. While a workout is recording, iOS shows a location indicator and the route keeps going with the screen locked.

Steps show as unavailable

Turn on iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → Fitness Tracking, and allow THRVE in that list.

Apple Health workouts don't appear

Open the Health app → your profile → Apps → THRVE and turn on the data types you want THRVE to read, then import again from THRVE.

Saving workouts to Apple Health

Turn on Save to Apple Health in THRVE Settings. Only workouts you record in THRVE are saved; imported ones are never copied back.

Pairing a heart rate strap or sensor

Open Settings → Sensors, allow Bluetooth, wear or spin the sensor so it wakes up, then tap Pair. Standard Bluetooth heart rate, cycling speed and cadence, and running speed and cadence sensors are supported.

Importing from Strava or files

Request your archive from Strava (Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account), then in THRVE open History → Import and pick the .zip. You can pick GPX, TCX and FIT files the same way.

Exporting my data

Open an activity and share it as GPX, or tap History → Export all to get everything as a .zip. Nothing is uploaded unless you send the file somewhere yourself.

Coach and search aren't showing

These use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. They appear on supported iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on in iOS Settings. Everything else works without it.

Privacy

THRVE collects no data. See the Privacy Policy.
